ABOUT VEDASIC

Democratizing hands-on silicon design.

VedASIC is proposed as a cloud-native, open-source web IDE for VLSI design and RTL verification. The project focuses on reducing the access barrier around practical hardware design workflows.

The problem

Professional EDA environments can be expensive and hardware-intensive, while student workflows are often split across separate tools and lab systems.

The solution

A browser workspace where users can author RTL, compile, simulate, inspect diagnostics and visualize results without rebuilding the environment each time.

The roadmap

Containerized execution, synthesis with Yosys, physical design with OpenLane, and Sky130-based RTL-to-GDSII exploration.

TEAM

Built for learning, prototyping and experimentation.

VedASIC is designed around a simple idea: make the hardware design flow easier to enter while keeping the engineering underneath real.
